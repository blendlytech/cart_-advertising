# Realtor Cold Call Script

**Source File:** [Realtor Scipt.txt](file:///c:/Users/DELL/cart_advertising/docs/Realtor%20Scipt.txt)

---

My Name is X and I got your name from Realtor.com. (Pause and wait for a response)

The reason why I called you is that I work with all the (Safeway/Savemart stores) in the area, and currently I'm working with the store on (X & Y street). Now are you familiar with that store? (Pause and wait for a response)

Well, what I'm doing with the store is I'm putting together a new customer referral program, and one of the categories we would like to have is a good realtor that we could recommend to all of the many thousands of happy shoppers that shop here. Now I'm curious are you more of a buyers agent or a listing agent? (Pause and wait for response)

How long have you been selling homes for? (Pause and wait for response).

Wow what's your secret? (Pause and wait)

I also heard there's a shortage of inventory here, is that true? (Pause and wait)

So does that make your job easier if you're the listing agent? (Pause and wait)

Well, the reason I was asking how long you've been selling homes for is it's really important to us and the store that we have the right realtor on there. So what I would like to do is give you the opportunity to take a look at our program, and see if it might be a good fit. Now, I have an availability at X or Y time to show you how it works. Which time would be best for you?

CONFIRM ADDRESS OF OFFICE YOUR MEETING
