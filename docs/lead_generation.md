## Category Prospecting Profiles

### 1. Independent Auto Repair Shops
- **Why Call Them:** Strong neighborhood customer base, repeat service opportunities, and a straightforward reason to build local name recognition.
- **Key Qualification Criteria:**
  - Verify that the owner makes advertising decisions.
  - Confirm the shop currently has bay capacity and room for additional repair/service work.

### 2. Established Real Estate Agents & Small Teams
- **Why Call Them:** Personal visibility fits the format particularly well; individual agents can be directly reachable without gatekeeper friction.
- **Key Qualification Criteria:**
  - Actively farming/working the supermarket's surrounding neighborhoods.
  - Already investing in local marketing, direct mail, or digital ads.

### 3. Locally Owned Insurance Agencies
- **Why Call Them:** A recognizable local agent can serve several household needs over time (auto, home, life).
- **Key Qualification Criteria:**
  - Has local advertising budget and decision-making authority.
  - Expresses clear interest in growing personal lines of insurance business.

### 4. Independent Family Dentists
- **Why Call Them:** Dental practices build long-lasting, multi-year relationships with nearby families and grocery shoppers.
- **Key Qualification Criteria:**
  - Actively accepting new patients.
  - Determine whether marketing decisions are made directly by the dentist or by the practice/office manager.

### 5. Residential HVAC, Plumbing & Pest Control Companies
- **Why Call Them:** Essential household services where being top-of-mind before an emergency or seasonal issue occurs has a clear rationale.
- **Key Qualification Criteria:**
  - Actively services the supermarket's primary trade area.
  - Has operational technician capacity to take on new customer accounts.

### 6. Pet Grooming & Boarding Businesses
- **Why Call Them:** Neighborhood proximity, convenience, and recurring appointments make a highly intuitive pitch.
- **Key Qualification Criteria:**
  - Has open appointment slots and capacity for growth.
  - Avoid facilities that are already fully booked and turning away clients.

### 7. Salons & Barbershops
- **Why Call Them:** Local ownership, hyper-local foot traffic, and high-frequency repeat visits make them worth testing.
- **Key Qualification Criteria:**
  - Has open chairs/booths, adequate staffing, and available budget to benefit from volume growth.

### 8. Independent Restaurants & Pizza Shops
- **Why Call Them:** Easy neighborhood connection and impulse appeal, especially when located within immediate radius of the grocery store.
- **Key Qualification Criteria:**
  - Willingness to invest in ongoing brand awareness.
  - Realistic expectations regarding long-term branding vs. immediate coupon response.
