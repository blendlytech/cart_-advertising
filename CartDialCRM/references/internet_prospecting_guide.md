## 7. Prospecting with Lycos (`www.lycos.com`)

1. Go to [www.lycos.com](https://www.lycos.com)
2. Click on the **Local** tab
3. Click **Distance**
4. In the **Key Word** box enter category
5. In the **Location** box enter store address

> **NOTE:** You can change the category in the Find Box without starting over.
